# Hardware Requirements

## 1. Cluster Composition

1. Each team must construct a cluster containing at least six compute nodes of the same model. Additional nodes may be heterogeneous, and RAM and storage capacities may differ between nodes.

2. Spare compute nodes are permitted, provided they comply with hardware requirements and are included in the budget.

3. Devices with socketed system RAM are prohibited.

4. Apple M-series and A-series processors are prohibited.

## 2. Laptop-Derived Hardware

1. Traditional laptops are prohibited by default.

2. Laptop-derived systems substantially equivalent to existing SBCs may be approved on a case-by-case basis at the organizing committee's discretion. Similarity to an SBC does not guarantee approval.

## 3. Hardware Budget and Pricing

1. The total hardware budget must not exceed USD $6,000, including compute, networking, storage, cooling, and other equipment necessary for cluster operation.

2. Secondhand, sponsored, donated, and previously owned hardware is permitted but must be assigned a reasonable market value.

3. Teams using secondhand market pricing must provide at least three comparable listings supporting the declared price.

4. International teams may use local market prices converted to USD.

## 4. Power Limits

1. The total wall-power consumption of all cluster equipment, including compute nodes, networking, storage, cooling, and peripherals, must remain below 300 W.

2. Each physical processor or SoC package must have a rated Thermal Design Power (TDP) not exceeding 20 W. This limit excludes components external to the processor package.

3. Processors or SoCs without publicly documented TDP ratings require committee approval based on supporting technical documentation.

## 5. Power Monitoring

1. Teams must continuously monitor the combined wall-power consumption of all competition equipment using a smart wall plug or equivalent monitoring device.

2. A web-accessible monitoring interface, such as Grafana, is encouraged but optional.

3. Teams requiring a separately purchased smart wall plug should contact the organizing committee.

## 6. Hardware Proposals and Modifications

1. Teams must submit hardware proposals for committee approval, identifying component models, specifications, prices, and processor or SoC TDP values.

2. Proposals must demonstrate compliance with cluster composition, hardware budget, and power requirements.

3. Teams may submit modifications within two weeks following proposal approval.
